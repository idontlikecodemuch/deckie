# Quick start storyboard

Desk's opening video (D46): 57.6 seconds, silent, captions in the picture, one path. The DJ starts it from the card on
the first screen, or from the Help menu (Mac) or the "⋯" menu (Windows and Linux); it does not play by itself. The
pictures of Desk are the real Windows and Linux window, captured by `capture/live.mjs` against today's pages and
today's engine on twelve generated songs; the iPad is a picture of Deckie made for this film in the iPad simulator
(`capture/ipad.sh`), naming a song of the crate file the capture exported. The window sits on the left, the step and
its caption on the right. The pointer is drawn where the capture really clicked.

Words follow `docs/product/voice.md`: sentence case, "songs", a crate is the songs and a crate file is the file, no
"AI". Lines are broken by phrase, a few words to a line, never one word alone, and a new sentence starts a line.
Pacifico for "you", "your" and "Deckie" only. The same film plays on the Mac, so no caption says "this computer" or
names a button only one window has (see "For the Mac" below).

**Why these beats.** The film teaches the one path a new DJ needs (research AG): add music, let Desk read it, get
the crate file to the iPad, use it beside the decks. Since D42 to D44 a DJ with no iPad can listen with Desk itself,
so the last beat shows Booth in Desk, on its violet ground, as the way for them; it is not a fifth step, so its label
is "No iPad?" with no number. Each caption says what the picture shows and one thing the picture cannot (that Stop
keeps what was read, that the file is small, that the picks are the iPad's).

**Where the words come from.** "Desk reads every song", "tempo, key and bars" and "Songs already read stay in your
crate" are Desk's own words (DeskWords: `firstBody`, `stopRunHelp`, and since D46 `stopAnyTime` under the run's
count). "iCloud Drive, a stick or a cable" and "AirDrop" summarise the box the window shows (`toIPad*`). "No music
inside" is D21: a crate file holds what Desk found, never audio (the one exported in the capture is a few hundred
kilobytes for 12 songs and 25 MB of music). "The picks beside it are the iPad's own" is D42: one picks list, shared.
The film closes on a brand frame as the site's explainer film does (the name written from the same pen paths, then the
tagline), not on a sentence: a quick start ends on what Deckie is called. "Your music never leaves this computer"
stays on Desk's first screen, where it is said already; on the owner's decision of Nov 23, 2026, it left the film.

**For the Mac.** One film serves both for now: the Mac's own window can only be captured with the Mac unlocked, and
it was locked. The captions are true on a Mac: Export crate file is in the Mac's File menu; iCloud Drive, a stick
and a cable work from a Mac, and the note names AirDrop; Booth is a button in the Mac's toolbar. A Mac cut would
show the Mac window and its **Send to iPad** in step 3 (caption: "Send it to your iPad. / AirDrop is one click.").

**Not shown, on purpose.** Add CD and Add folder (named in the caption, not clicked: one way is enough to teach the
three), the question before a read of more than 1,000 songs, naming songs, Sources, the rekordbox route, several
crates, the lookup. A quick start shows the one path; the rest is found on the screens.
