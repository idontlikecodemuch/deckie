# Deckie explainer, fourth cut: shot list

76 s · 120 BPM (one beat is half a second, one bar is two) · 152 beats · 16:9, 1920 x 1080, 60 frames a second.
Music: a synthesised house bed made for the film (A minor): an eight-bar introduction under the first 16 s, the full
beat from the first section, a resolve under the last line. No voice. Small sounds mark the steps. Mixed to -14 LUFS.

Headlines are Inter ExtraBold, sentence case; smaller lines Inter. Three words are set in Pacifico in headline copy
and written on screen stroke by stroke: *you*, *your* (accent colour) and *Deckie* where it stands alone as the
product's name. They are marked *like this*. There are three named sections, each with a header in Inter at one
fixed place (baseline y = 322, 44 px): "Deckie Desk, on your Mac", "Deckie Booth, on your iPad", "The dictionary".
No boxed stamps. Every screen is a capture of the real apps showing a demo crate file (real analysis, invented
names). The first 15 s are also cut as a clip of their own.

The reasons are in `docs/research/v-explainer-video-and-page.md`, sections 7c and 7d. Earlier cuts: `v1/` (84 s),
`v2/` (60 s), `v3/` (76 s, before round six).
