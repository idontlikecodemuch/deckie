# Deckie explainer, fifth cut: shot list

76 s · 120 BPM (one beat is half a second, one bar is two) · 152 beats · 16:9, 1920 x 1080, 60 frames a second.
Sound: soundtrack D, "in between" (letter V, section 7g): a swung house groove with organ stabs, heard through a
filter that opens over the first 16 s, the drop on the one, then DJ moves at the section changes; the accents are
percussion played in the groove. The sound column below still describes the earlier bed's accents. No voice.
-14 LUFS.

Headlines are Inter ExtraBold, sentence case; smaller lines Inter. Three words are set in Pacifico in headline copy
and written on screen stroke by stroke: *you*, *your* (accent colour) and *Deckie* where it stands alone as the
product's name. They are marked *like this*. There are three named sections, each with a white header at one
fixed place (baseline y = 322, 48 px): "Deckie Desk on your Mac", "Deckie Booth on your iPad", "The dictionary"
(the qualifier "on your Mac" is smaller, 34 px, and grey). The app names "Deckie Desk" and "Deckie Booth" in the headers are in Pacifico and written once, as the section
opens. Every line break is set by hand: no line of one word, terms unbroken, a new sentence starts a line (a slash
below marks a break).
No boxed stamps. Every screen is a capture of the real apps showing a demo crate file (real analysis, invented
names). The first 15 s are also cut as a clip of their own.

The reasons are in `docs/research/v-explainer-video-and-page.md`, sections 7c to 7e. Earlier cuts: `v1/` (84 s),
`v2/` (60 s), `v3/` and `v4/` (76 s, before rounds six and seven).
