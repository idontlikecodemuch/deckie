# Deckie explainer: shot list

84 s · 120 BPM (one beat is half a second, one bar is two) · 168 beats · 16:9, 1920 x 1080, 60 frames a second.
Music: a synthesised house bed made for the film (A minor), soft for the first 16 s, full from step 1, resolving
under the last line. No voice. Small sounds mark the steps. Mixed to -14 LUFS.

Every screen is a capture of the real apps showing a demo crate file (real analysis, invented names). Captions sit in
the left column; the screen sits in a rounded card on the right, and the camera pushes in to the line being talked
about.
