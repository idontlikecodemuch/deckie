# Deckie explainer, second cut: shot list

60 s · 120 BPM (one beat is half a second, one bar is two) · 120 beats · 16:9, 1920 x 1080, 60 frames a second.
Music: a synthesised house bed made for the film (A minor): an eight-bar introduction under the first 16 s, the full
beat from step 1, a resolve under the last line. No voice. Small sounds mark the steps. Mixed to -14 LUFS.

Titles, stamps and labels are set in Inter ExtraBold; smaller lines in Inter; the name "Deckie" and the words about
the DJ ("your music.", "your crate.", "You mix.", "the one you want.", "Your music") in Pacifico, in the accent colour.
The address deckie.io is printed on the name card (from 14 s) and on the end card. Every screen is a capture of the real
apps showing a demo crate file (real analysis, invented names). The first 15 s are also cut as a clip of their own.
The reasons for each shot and each word are in `docs/research/v-explainer-video-and-page.md`, sections 4 and 5. The
first cut (84 s) is in `v1/`.
