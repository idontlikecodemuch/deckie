# Deckie explainer, third cut: shot list

76 s · 120 BPM (one beat is half a second, one bar is two) · 152 beats · 16:9, 1920 x 1080, 60 frames a second.
Music: a synthesised house bed made for the film (A minor): an eight-bar introduction under the first 16 s, the full
beat from step 1, a resolve under the last line. No voice. Small sounds mark the steps. Mixed to -14 LUFS.

Headlines, stamps and labels are Inter ExtraBold; smaller lines Inter. Three words are set in Pacifico wherever they
stand in a headline, and each is written on screen stroke by stroke: *you*, *your* (accent colour) and *Deckie* (the
colour of its line). They are marked *like this* below. Every screen is a capture of the real apps showing a demo
crate file (real analysis, invented names). The first 15 s are also cut as a clip of their own.

The reasons for each shot, the rule for the script words and the stroke plans are in
`docs/research/v-explainer-video-and-page.md`, section 7c. Earlier cuts: `v1/` (84 s) and `v2/` (60 s).
