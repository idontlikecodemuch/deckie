Privacy

Your music.
Your mix.

Deckie is made so that your music, and what you play, stay on your own devices. The one exception is a lookup you ask for, said in full below.

Deckie Booth and Deckie Desk, by App Hold Media.
Effective November 19, 2026.

What we collect: nothing

Deckie has no account and no sign-in. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not receive your music, your crate files, your set recordings or anything about how you use the apps.

Deckie Booth, on the iPad

Booth does not use the internet at all.

It uses the iPad’s microphone to recognise which of your songs is playing. The sound is worked on, on the iPad, and never leaves it.

Booth also records each set from the microphone, so you can keep it. The recording, the list of songs Booth named and a log are saved on the iPad, in the Files app under Deckie Booth, and nowhere else. You can delete them there at any time.

The links in Booth, to deckie.io and to where the demo crate’s songs came from, open in Safari when you tap them.

Deckie Desk, on your computer

Desk reads your music on your computer and writes a crate file: what it learns about your songs, never the songs. The crate file goes only where you send it, such as to your iPad by AirDrop or Files.

Everything else Desk does stays on your computer, with the one exception below.

Looking up songs, on a Mac

Desk for Mac can look up songs in MusicBrainz, a public music encyclopedia, to fill in names it does not have. It does this only when you ask. Before anything is sent, Desk shows exactly what will go, and nothing goes until you say yes.

What is sent, and nothing more:

  • For a song: its title, artist and release, as written in Desk. If its file carries an ISRC (the recording’s serial number), that is sent first. A song with no title sends the words of its file name, not where the file is.
  • For a music CD: the lengths of its songs, to ask which disc it is; if that finds nothing, the disc’s name.
  • When a song fits, a request for the label of its release.
  • With every request: the app’s name and version and the address deckie.io, as MusicBrainz asks of every app. Like any website, MusicBrainz also sees your internet address.

The songs themselves are never sent, and neither is where your files are, your other songs or anything about your computer. Desk lists every request it has made under “What Desk has sent”. What MusicBrainz does with a request is covered by its own privacy policy.

Deckie Desk on Windows and Linux

Desk for Windows and Linux cannot look songs up yet. It sends nothing over the internet.

This site

deckie.io sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. The demo crate’s songs play from this site itself.

Children

Deckie is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and say so in the apps before it takes effect.
