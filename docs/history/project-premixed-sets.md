FINAL (evening of Oct 24, 2026): after Chris's listening rounds the three hours were replaced by TWO ~72 min mixes, "1 - Haunted Disco" (pop/Rihanna lean) and "2 - Witching Hour Warehouse" (harder/Halloween), joined into ONE continuous file `mixes/Halloween Mix - full.mp3` (144:40, 347 MB, -10 LUFS) with `.cue`/`.txt`, built as an MP3 CD ISO at `CDs mix/ISO/Halloween Mix - full.iso` (volume HALLOWEEN MIX, mount-verified) by `build_mix_discs.py`; Chris burns it elsewhere with the usual hdiutil command (`CDs v3/verify-mix-disc.sh` checks a burn). The mixes are remix-forward dance with 3-4 callouts (brake/echo/backspin/slam into a slow or pop moment) and an 8-10 min hip-hop quick-mix stretch per half with slams, chops and scratch stabs; transitions follow `transition-recipes.md` (DJ practice research) plus teaser stabs and a seeded human-variance layer; all 27 of Chris's named picks and his individually downloaded remixes are in; Running Up That Hill kept, Von Dutch and Cotton Eye Joe out. Earlier history: `plan.py` and `render.py` (plus `sampler.py`) first rendered three ~60 min sets into `_pool build/mixer/mixes/`: "1 - Graveyard Shift" (spookier), "2 - Haunted Block Party" (hip-hop lean), "3 - Mirrorball Massacre" (disco lean), each a climb from ~87 to ~130 BPM through hip-hop, pop/disco and dance stages with 5-6 Halloween songs, CBR 320, -10 LUFS to match the discs, with .cue, .txt tracklist and .render.json per set, plus `preview.mp3` (hour 1, first 4 joins) and `transition-examples.mp3` (12 clips, one per transition type). Plans are editable (`plan_<n>.json`) and re-render in 3-4 min per hour. Source: the 234 off-disc candidates plus 24 library Halloween tracks added that day (candidates.json now 371, `extra_lib_ids.json`).

**Why:** fill-in music for when Mike is not playing; the party was that night, so the sets were rendered before anyone listened.
