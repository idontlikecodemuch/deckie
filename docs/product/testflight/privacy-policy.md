# Privacy policy (draft, for deckie.io/privacy)

**Deckie Booth and Deckie Desk, by App Hold Media. Effective [DATE].**

Your music. Your mix. Deckie is made so that nothing about you or your music leaves your devices.

**What we collect: nothing.** Deckie has no account and no sign-in. It has no analytics, no advertising and no
tracking. We do not receive your music, your crate files, your recordings or anything about how you use the apps.

**The microphone.** Deckie Booth uses the iPad's microphone to recognise which of your songs is playing. The sound is
worked on, on the iPad, and never leaves it. Booth also records your set from the microphone so you can keep it; the
recording is saved on the iPad, in the Files app under Deckie Booth, and nowhere else. You can delete it there at
any time.

**Your music and crate files.** Deckie Desk reads your music on your computer and writes a crate file: what it learns
about your songs, never the songs. The crate file goes only where you send it, such as to your iPad by AirDrop or
Files.

**Looking up a song's details.** If you ask Deckie Desk to look up songs it could not name, it sends the words or the
sound fingerprint needed for that lookup to MusicBrainz and AcoustID, public music databases, and nothing else. It
does this only when you ask. [CHECK BEFORE PUBLISHING: whether the lookup ships in the Desk testers get.]

**Children.** Deckie is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

**Changes.** If this ever changes, we will update this page and say so in the apps before it takes effect.
