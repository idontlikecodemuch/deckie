# Privacy policy (for deckie.io/privacy)

The words of `site/privacy/index.html`, published as https://deckie.io/privacy. Change them here and on the page
together. Checked against the code on Nov 19, 2026: Deckie (`deck-app`) names no networking API and its
privacy manifest declares no collected data; the only network code is `core/Sources/DeckieLookup`, MusicBrainz on
the Mac only (not built on Windows and Linux); what a lookup sends is `MusicBrainz.swift` and the `lookupSend*`
lines in `DeskWords.swift`. AcoustID is in no app a tester gets and is not named.

**Deckie on the iPad (until build 2 called Deckie Booth) and Deckie Desk, by App Hold Media. Effective November 19, 2026.**

Your music. Your mix. Deckie is made so that your music, and what you play, stay on your own devices. The one
exception is a lookup you ask for, said in full below.

**What we collect: nothing.** Deckie has no account and no sign-in. It has no analytics, no advertising and no
tracking. We do not receive your music, your crate files, your set recordings or anything about how you use the
apps.

**Deckie, on the iPad.** Deckie does not use the internet at all. It uses the iPad's microphone to recognise
which of your songs is playing. The sound is worked on, on the iPad, and never leaves it. Deckie also records each set
from the microphone, so you can keep it. The recording, the list of songs Deckie named and a log are saved on the
iPad, in the Files app under Deckie, and nowhere else. You can delete them there at any time. The links in
Deckie, to deckie.io and to where the demo crate's songs came from, open in Safari when you tap them.

**Deckie Desk, on your computer.** Desk reads your music on your computer and writes a crate file: what it learns
about your songs, never the songs. The crate file goes only where you send it, such as to your iPad by AirDrop or
Files. Everything else Desk does stays on your computer, with the one exception below.

**Looking up songs, on a Mac.** Desk for Mac can look up songs in MusicBrainz, a public music encyclopedia, to fill
in names it does not have. It does this only when you ask. Before anything is sent, Desk shows exactly what will go,
and nothing goes until you say yes. What is sent, and nothing more:

- For a song: its title, artist and release, as written in Desk. If its file carries an ISRC (the recording's serial
  number), that is sent first. A song with no title sends the words of its file name, not where the file is.
- For a music CD: the lengths of its songs, to ask which disc it is; if that finds nothing, the disc's name.
- When a song fits, a request for the label of its release.
- With every request: the app's name and version and the address deckie.io, as MusicBrainz asks of every app. Like
  any website, MusicBrainz also sees your internet address.

The songs themselves are never sent, and neither is where your files are, your other songs or anything about your
computer. Desk lists every request it has made under "What Desk has sent". What MusicBrainz does with a request is
covered by its own privacy policy.

**Deckie Desk on Windows and Linux.** Desk for Windows and Linux cannot look songs up yet. It sends nothing over the
internet.

**This site.** deckie.io sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. The demo crate's
songs play from this site itself.

**Children.** Deckie is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

**Changes.** If this ever changes, we will update this page and say so in the apps before it takes effect.

**Contact.** App Hold Media, info@apphold.media.
