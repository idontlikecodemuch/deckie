# What to test, build 2 (draft)

Thanks for trying Deckie on the iPad. New in this build: the app is called Deckie, there is a Stop button, and the
demo crate has new songs that go together. Please try these and tell us how each went:

1. **The demo crate.** Tap "Try the demo crate", then on a phone or laptop open deckie.io/demo, press play and put it
   near the iPad. How soon does Deckie name each song? When you tap another song on the page, how many seconds until
   Deckie shows it?
2. **The picks.** With a demo song named, would you play the picks next? Most should be the same key or the next key
   round the wheel; one song (Street of love) is there to show a key clash.
3. **Stop.** Tap Stop in the corner of the Listen screen. Does the microphone turn off, and does it say the set is
   saved? Find the set in Files, On My iPad > Deckie > Sets. Tap Start listening: does it pick up again?
4. **Getting a crate file in.** Make one with Deckie Desk, send it to the iPad by AirDrop or save it to Files, then
   open it (tap "Open a crate file", or open it from Files). Did it open? How long did it take?
5. **Naming the song.** Play from your crate in the room. Does Deckie name the right song, and how soon? Does it keep
   up when you mix from one song to the next?
6. **Locked iPad.** Lock the iPad during a set and unlock it later. Did it keep listening, and is the set's recording
   complete in Files under Deckie > Sets?
7. **Setting a song by hand.** When the room is too loud, tap a song in the Crate tab. Do the picks follow?
8. **Recording on and off.** Tap REC and untick "Record each set". Does it say "REC off", and does it stay off after
   you close and reopen Deckie?

Known in this build:

- The demo crate's seven songs are public-domain dance music, chosen to show the picks working, not as a DJ's crate.
- Switching songs on the demo page can take 6 to 8 seconds to show on the iPad; we are working on it.
- An update from build 1 keeps your sets; the app's folder in Files is now called Deckie.
- A crate file made on a Windows PC has no song fingerprints yet, so Deckie cannot name songs from it: set them by
  hand in the Crate tab.
- The look and the words are still being worked on.
