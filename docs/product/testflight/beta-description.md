# Beta app description (draft)

Deckie Booth sits beside the decks. It listens to the room, names the song that's playing from your own crate,
and shows what could come next and how to mix it in: the pitch to set, how the keys sit, and a move that could
work.

It works from a crate file made by Deckie Desk on your Mac or Windows PC. Desk reads your music and writes what it
learns about your songs, never the songs themselves. Send the crate file to the iPad by AirDrop or Files and open
it in Booth.

Everything happens on the iPad. The microphone's sound never leaves it, there is no account, and it works with no
signal. Each set is recorded on the iPad for you to keep, in Files under Deckie Booth; tap REC to turn
recording off.

This is an early beta. Tell us what it named right, what it missed, and which picks you would actually play.
