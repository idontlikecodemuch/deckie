# Name check (Oct 26, 2026): Fader, Cue, Cue Sheet, plus how the field names itself and five new names

Method: US-only snippet web search; no direct App Store, Google Play, USPTO or EUIPO access; domains DNS-only (empty record = "probably free", needs a registrar check). Run USPTO Trademark Search and EUIPO eSearch plus a lawyer's clearance before committing.

## Fader
- Stores: no DJ app named exactly Fader. Close: "Fader - System Audio Mixer" (Mac per-app volume); Mixfader dj app (MWM, hardware-tied); Mackie My Fader (live-sound remote); Yamaha Faders (discontinued 2020). Google Play unchecked.
- Trademark: The FADER magazine/label (since 1999): THE FADER LABEL registered and renewed (class 9 recordings); FADER filed April 2022 class 41 online music magazine; older FADER RECORDS filings; cancelled PRO SCRATCH FADER (audio crossfader). FADERPRO class 42 (2021). Generic mixing term, weak as a mark, and partly owned in music classes by a well-known publisher. EUIPO not found (UNVERIFIED).
- Domains: fader.app parked/for sale; fader.io "coming soon" parking; getfader.com and faderapp.com registered; fader.dj no records (probably free, UNVERIFIED).
- DJ usage: the hardware slider (channel, crossfader, pitch fader). DJs would assume a mixer/controller utility. Fits the pitch reading, mismatches the rest.
- Score: 1 / 1 / 3. Verdict: avoid.

## Cue and Cue Sheet
- Stores: many unrelated Cue apps (Wonder Workshop robot, KU Leuven metronome, CUE Astrology, CUE Band for Parkinson's, appCUE building automation, Cue Master theatre player, Cue Play DJ 2011 possibly dormant, Cue Health). Full store listings unchecked.
- Trademark: CUE-formative marks held crowded and weak by a federal court (Cue Acoustics v. GM); active filings in medical, Parkinson's app, calendar apps; CUE+ registered 2023. "Cue sheet" is the industry's sync-licensing paperwork. EUIPO not found.
- Domains: cue.app, cue.dj (redirects to cue.live), cue.io, getcue.com, cueapp.com all registered; cuesheet.app (SyncCraft stage-program tool, coming soon), cuesheet.io, getcuesheet.com, cuesheetapp.com registered; cuesheet.dj no records (probably free, UNVERIFIED).
- DJ usage: core vocabulary (cue points, hot cues, headphone cueing); DJs would expect a cueing tool. "Cue sheet" reads as a licensing document.
- Connotations: one syllable; queue/Q homophone spelling risk.
- Scores: Cue 1 / 1 / 4 (taken, hard to own). Cue Sheet 2 / 2 / 3 (descriptive of the tracklist only; .app and .com occupied).

## How the field names itself
- DJ software: rekordbox, Serato DJ (Pyro, Studio), Traktor, djay, VirtualDJ, Mixxx, Engine DJ, Beatport DJ, Soda, DJ Player Pro, Hook, Pacemaker, Mixfader dj.
- Library/prep: Lexicon DJ, Rekordcloud, Mixo (mixo.dj), Mixed In Key, DJ.Studio.
- Track ID: Beatport Track ID (May 2026, powered by seeqnc), Shazam, 1001Tracklists; EarShot and RaveDJ not found in searches (UNVERIFIED).
- Patterns: (1) compounds fused with "mix" or "DJ" (rekordbox, VirtualDJ, Mixxx, Mixo, Mixed In Key); (2) DJ prefix/suffix (DJ.Studio, Lexicon DJ, Engine DJ, djay); (3) feature-literal names (Track ID, Cue Play); (4) clipped invented words in -o/-x/-ix (Mixo, Mixxx, Traktor, Serato); (5) single common nouns are rare (Lexicon, Pacemaker, Soda, Hook), and the obvious ones (Fader, Cue, Crate, Deck) are taken or generic.
- Empty spaces: names for the role or relationship (partner, second pair of ears, co-pilot); booth/room/set-as-journey vocabulary (arc, handoff, phrase, blend) rather than "mix"; plain words from outside music (spotter, lookahead); no "DJ" in the name at all (about half the field uses it), with .dj as a secondary domain.

## Five new names (DNS only; no store or trademark checks)
1. Segue: moving smoothly to the next track; names the job. segue.app and segue.io registered (segue.io for sale); segue.dj probably free. Risk HIGH (Apple's UI term, Segway sound-alike, store crowding); use only with a qualifier.
2. Spotter: from lifting and climbing, the person beside you who calls what's coming and catches mistakes. spotter.app/.io registered; spotter.dj probably free. Risk MEDIUM-HIGH (other apps and businesses).
3. Lookahead: audio engines use it for seeing what comes next. lookahead.app/.io registered; lookahead.dj probably free. Risk MEDIUM (generic; construction-scheduling meaning).
4. Overlap: the overlap of two tracks in a mix. overlap.dj registered (live site, not fetched); overlap.io registered; overlap.app no records (probably free). Risk MEDIUM.
5. Second Chair: orchestra phrase for the supporting player beside the lead; keeps the DJ in charge; empty in the field. secondchair.app registered (parked); secondchair.dj and secondchair.io no records (probably free). Risk LOW-MEDIUM (used in law and healthcare); two words.

Recommendation from this agent: drop Fader and Cue; prioritise Second Chair and Overlap for full checks; Spotter as backup; consider a two-word mark for a cleaner register.
