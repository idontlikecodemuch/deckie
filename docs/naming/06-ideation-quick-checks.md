## Corrections from the deep check (05-deckie.md)

- deckie.app is a duck-themed flashcard web app, not a personal page.
- decki.io, decki.net and decki.co are registered (June 2026); the table above listing decki.io as free is wrong.
- An iOS app named exactly "Decki" (Brazilian travel diary, deckiapp.com) exists in the GB, FR and BR storefronts.
- DECKEE is a registered mark, not an application: US Reg. 6993660 (7 Mar 2023, narrow boating-safety goods), IR 1605323
  protected in the EU, UK, NZ, Canada and Jersey with broad goods ("computer software, downloadable software (apps)"),
  Australian registrations 2119248 and 2119251 (2020).
- deckie.com is listed on Atom at $59,888 (or $2,500 down plus $2,750 a month for 24 months).
- The bare "deckie" handles on SoundCloud, Mixcloud and YouTube are dormant non-DJ accounts; "deckieapp" is free on
  Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Bluesky, SoundCloud, Mixcloud, Bandcamp, Twitch, GitHub, npm and PyPI.
