# Product name: ranked shortlist (Oct 26, 2026)

Three low-level agents checked the App Store (live iTunes Search API, US), domains (DNS/RDAP/HTTP only) and
trademark aggregators (not the registers themselves) for the owner's names and ten of ours, and surveyed how the
field names itself. Their reports are `01-`, `02-` and `03-` in this folder. Scores are distinctiveness /
availability / fit, each 1-5. Nothing here is a clearance: before committing, run USPTO Trademark Search and
EUIPO eSearch for classes 9, 41 and 42, check Google Play, and confirm .dj and .app at a registrar.

## What the field looks like

Most DJ products fuse "mix" or "DJ" into the name (rekordbox, VirtualDJ, Mixxx, Mixo, Mixed In Key, Engine DJ,
DJ.Studio), or clip an invented word (Traktor, Serato). Single common nouns are rare and the obvious ones (Deck,
Fader, Cue, Crate, Booth) are taken or generic. The open ground is names for the role (a second pair of ears, the
helper beside the DJ) and words from outside music. A name without "DJ" in it stands out; .dj works as the secondary
domain.

## Recommendation

Deckhand, with a descriptive App Store subtitle ("Deckhand: DJ Set Assistant") and deckhand.dj as the home. It
keeps the word the owner likes, tells a DJ what it is in one glance, and sits in the field's open ground (the
helper, not the star). Earmark is the alternative if the Deckhand trademark search turns up a class 9/42 conflict.
Second Chair is the creative wildcard: cleanest register, but two words and no DJ connotation.

Before the name goes into the brief: knockout trademark search for DECKHAND in classes 9, 41, 42 (USPTO, EUIPO),
a registrar check of deckhand.dj, and a Google Play search.

## Update (later on Oct 26, 2026)

The owner chose Deckhand and asked for a deep check of Deckhand, DJ Deckhand and Deckhand DJ. That report is
`04-deckhand-variants.md`: weighted totals Deckhand 71.4, Deckhand DJ 68.0, DJ Deckhand 66.6; recommendation is the
brand "Deckhand" with "DJ" in the subtitle, domain (deckhand.dj) and handle (deckhandapp). Decisive fact: a live US
registration DECKHAND (Reg. 6629861, Real Time Data Pty Ltd, fishing e-logbook software, classes 9 and 42) needs an
attorney's knockout opinion before any filing. The "Deckhand ops SaaS on Capterra" in report 01 is that same company.

## Decision (evening of Oct 26, 2026): Deckie

The DECKHAND registration gave the owner pause ("a VC backed software called deckhand gives me pause"), so the
session ideated on words that accompany "deck" and on "queue"; the quick checks are in `06-ideation-quick-checks.md`.
Principle that sorted most ideas: examiners compare marks on sight, sound and meaning, so respellings (Deck-and,
Deck&, Deckand) and prefixes (Next Deckhand, mydeckhand, Qdeckhand) that keep the sound of DECKHAND inherit its
problem; only a different word moves the risk.

Final top three: **1. Deckie** (Australian/NZ slang for a deckhand; clean on both stores; deckie.dj and deckie.io
open; handle deckieapp free on SoundCloud/Mixcloud/YouTube/GitHub; open item: the sound-alike DECKEE boating mark),
**2. Deckhand** (best fit, pending an attorney's read on Reg. 6629861), **3. Deckside** (clean distance, least
descriptive). Alternate: Third Deck. The owner chose Deckie; Decki is the coined-spelling variant kept in reserve.
Deep report: `05-deckie.md`.

Deep check result (`05-deckie.md`, same rubric as 04): Deckie 72.2, Deckhand 71.4, Decki 64.4. Deckie moves the
trademark problem rather than escaping it: DECKEE (boating-safety app, Deckee Pty Ltd) is registered for class 9
software in the US (narrow goods), and in the EU, UK, Australia, NZ and Canada with broad goods. US exposure is about
the same as Deckhand's; UK/EU/AU exposure is worse. Cheap, reversible purchases (deckie.dj, deckie.io, getdeckie.com,
handle deckieapp) are fine while the attorney reviews DECKEE; hold deckie.com ($59,888), any filing and any public
launch until then. Ways out the attorney should assess: a coexistence agreement limited to DJ software, or partial
non-use cancellation of DECKEE's broad "computer software" claim (possible in the EU/UK from about Jan 2027).
