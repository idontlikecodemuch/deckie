# Deckhand, DJ Deckhand, Deckhand DJ: naming investigation (Oct 26, 2026)

Research only. Nothing was registered, no account was created, no terms were accepted, no site was logged into.
Every fact is tagged **[V]** (verified by a tool named in the text) or **[U]** (unverified: aggregator, search
snippet, blocked site or general knowledge). This is not legal clearance; a trademark attorney's search is still
needed before filing.

## 1. Summary and recommendation

**Ranking (weighted, out of 100): 1. Deckhand 71.4 · 2. Deckhand DJ 68.0 · 3. DJ Deckhand 66.6.**

Use **Deckhand** as the brand. Put "DJ" in the App Store subtitle and in the domain, not in the name:

- App Store name "Deckhand: DJ Booth Companion" (28 characters); subtitle "Track ID, next track, the join" (30).
- Home on **deckhand.dj** (no DNS at the .dj registry; still needs a registrar check), with **djdeckhand.com** and
  **deckhanddj.com** (both unregistered at Verisign) bought as cheap redirects.
- One handle across platforms: **deckhandapp** (free on X, TikTok, YouTube, Bluesky, SoundCloud, Mixcloud,
  Bandcamp, GitHub, npm, PyPI; Instagram, Threads, Facebook and Reddit could not be checked).

The DJ variants score better on availability (free .com, free handles, no exact store collision) but lose on the
things that decide whether DJs like the product. "DJ Deckhand" reads as a performer's alias (the "DJ Shadow"
pattern), which names the app as the DJ and cuts against "helper, never autopilot"; it also sounds like the toy
mixer apps that fill the store under "DJ ...". "Deckhand DJ" follows the Engine DJ / Lexicon DJ pattern and is
the better of the two, but the exact handle @deckhanddj on YouTube already belongs to a DJ channel called
"DECKhand". Neither variant gets away from the trademark issue below, because "DJ" is descriptive for DJ software
and would be disclaimed; the dominant element is still DECKHAND.

**Three decisive facts**

1. **A live US registration for DECKHAND covers software in classes 9 and 42.** Reg. 6629861 (serial 88914917),
   Real Time Data Pty Ltd (Adelaide), registered 25 Jan 2022: electronic logbook software and SaaS for fishermen.
   The owner uses "Deckhand®" in its App Store listings and raised about $1.4M (Series A 2024, per CB Insights).
   The goods are different (fishing compliance vs DJ performance), so coexistence is plausible, but it is the
   identical word in the identical classes, and it applies equally to all three variants. [V: Trademarkia record;
   App Store listing text via iTunes API. U: CB Insights funding via search snippet.]
2. **No DJ or music app is named Deckhand on either store, and no Deckhand app is in Music.** Nine iOS apps carry
   the name across seven storefronts (boat maintenance, boat wash, fishing logbook x2, pool service, two card-game
   apps, a boat-records app, and an iPad show-control app); Google Play has one (a card game, 5+ downloads). [V:
   iTunes Search API, Google Play search pages via curl.]
3. **Hobby DJs already use the word.** SoundCloud /deckhand (Daniel McConnell, location "The Seven Seas", 21
   uploads incl. "NYE2025", "Easter Mix", "A Tivat Odyssey", 145 followers, profile updated Oct 2025) and YouTube
   @deckhanddj ("DECKhand", techno/tech house live sets, #PioneerDj, created 31 Dec 2022, no public videos in its
   feed). No one was found billing as "DJ Deckhand"; the exact phrase is dominated by yacht "Deckhand/DJ" job ads.
   [V: SoundCloud page, YouTube page and RSS feed via curl.]

**Biggest risk:** a USPTO section 2(d) refusal or an opposition from Real Time Data on a class 9/42 filing, the same
for every variant. Second: findability. "Deckhand" search results are job boards, dictionaries and fishing
software, so the brand will rank only with "DJ" attached.

**Correction to earlier notes:** the "Deckhand boat-operations SaaS on Capterra" in 01-/00- is the same Real Time
Data fishing logbook, not a separate company. "Deckhand!" is a boat-engine monitoring app, not generic utilities.

## 4. Findings per dimension

### 4.1 Linguistics and meaning
- **Pronunciation and spelling.** DECK-hand, two stressed syllables, no ambiguous letters. The one spelling doubt is
  spacing: Deckhand / Deck Hand / DeckHand. Dictionaries give both "deckhand" and "deck hand" [V: WebSearch
  results for dictionary.com and WordReference listing "deck hand"]. Pick "Deckhand" and register the spaced form
  as a redirect/handle. The DJ variants add a second doubt (DJ vs Dj vs dj, and "Deckhand DJ" heard as
  "deckhand.dj").
- **Meaning to a DJ.** "Deck" is the DJ's word for a CDJ or turntable; "hand" is help. Read together: the helper
  at the decks. The nautical reading (crew member who does the work on deck so the captain can steer) gives the
  same hierarchy the product wants: the DJ is the captain. Mild downside: a deckhand is junior crew, so the name
  undersells the intelligence; that is acceptable for the "never autopilot" positioning.
- **Other languages [U: general linguistic knowledge, not native-speaker checked].** Spanish: no meaning, "deck"
  is known from "deck de madera"; native speakers may soften the h ("dek-and"). French: "deck" is a loanword
  for a terrace; nothing awkward. German: "Deck" (ship's deck) and "Hand" are both German words, so it reads
  transparently as "deck hand"; nearby "Decke" (blanket/ceiling) is harmless. Portuguese: "deck" is a loanword;
  nothing awkward. Japanese: デッキハンド (dekki hando); デッキ is familiar from tape decks and ship decks. No
  vulgar or negative meanings found. English slang "deck someone" (punch) does not attach to the compound.
- **In a sentence.** "Ask Deckhand", "Deckhand says the next one's in A minor", "I run Deckhand on the iPad" all
  work. "Ask DJ Deckhand" sounds like asking another person in the booth, and "DJ Deckhand says..." sounds like a
  rival DJ.

### 4.2 Fit to positioning
- **Helper vs autopilot.** "Deckhand" names a role that serves someone else. "DJ Deckhand" names the app as a DJ,
  i.e. the performer; this is the autopilot reading the brief says DJs resent. "Deckhand DJ" reads either as
  "Deckhand, for DJs" (good, like Engine DJ, Lexicon DJ) or "a DJ that is a deckhand" (ambiguous).
- **Credibility.** The US App Store's top results for "dj deckhand" are DJ Mixer Studio, Cross DJ, DJ it!, Music
  DJ - Dance DJ, DJ Soundz [V: iTunes API, us, entity=software]. A "DJ ..." prefix puts the product in that
  crowd. Pro tools in BRIEF section 10 (rekordbox, Serato, Traktor, Lexicon, Mixed In Key) do not lead with "DJ".
- **App Store fields (30/30).** Options measured:
  - "Deckhand: DJ Booth Companion" (28) + "Track ID, next track, the join" (30)
  - "Deckhand: DJ Set Assistant" (26) + "Knows your crate. You pick." (27)
  - "DJ Deckhand" (11) leaves the name field without a descriptor; "DJ Deckhand: Booth Companion" (28)
  - "Deckhand DJ: Booth Companion" (28)
  Apple indexes name and subtitle for search, so "DJ" in the name/subtitle gives the same findability either
  way; the brand need not carry it. [U: Apple search indexing behaviour from general knowledge.]

### 4.9 Brand and extension
- **Wordmark.** "Deckhand" in a sturdy grotesque or a slightly condensed stencil; 8 letters with the
  d-k-h ascenders gives a strong silhouette.
- **Three logo directions.**
  1. *Cleat*: a boat cleat drawn so its two horns read as two platters/jog wheels; nautical, quiet, works at icon
     size.
  2. *Hand on the platter*: a simple open hand whose palm is a vinyl/jog circle, one finger touching the edge
     (nudge gesture); says "helper at the deck" without words.
